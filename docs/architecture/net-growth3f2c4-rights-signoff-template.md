# NET-GROWTH3.F.2C.4 — Rights sign-off worksheet (human counsel)

**Status:** `UNSIGNED / NOT AUTHORIZED`. This is a blank decision framework, not legal advice or a release permission.

## Specific use case

Smart Steel Sales proposes a public B2B Network of Italian corporate manufacturers and processors, with a minimum record comprising verified **legal-person** identity, official website, conservative industrial category, an original one-sentence fact about tube products or processing, dated links to each first-party source, and an opt-out/claim option. It does **not** include copied website descriptions, photos, logos, catalog dumps, named staff contacts, inferred grades/dimensions, live stock, customer data or supplier-verification badges.

| **Company first-party sites** | Website-specific terms, [EU database rights](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/protection-databases) | Minimal factual metadata, original succinct paraphrase, cite linked URLs | **PENDING counsel's intended-use and opt-out decision** |
| **Natural-person business data** | [European Commission GDPR guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/application-gdpr_en) | No person-level contact fields and review sole proprietorship risk | **PENDING classification/approval** |

**Counsel decision fields, per company:** legal name and VAT, permitted set of original factual fields, source terms/privacy assessment, notice/opt-out, entity-vs-person classification, approved scope, reviewer ID, decision date, private decision artifact reference, restrictions and reassessment triggers. All must be explicit. Open internet visibility is not proof of reuse permission; conversely, publicly available factual details are not automatically creative copyright content.

**Operational controls:** brand/owner duplicates quarantined; no scraping protected source databases in bulk; metadata only; no staff email/phone; search/AI confidence labels are distinct from verified supplier status; written approval before import; one authorized service-role ROLLBACK and independent no-write readback before any distinct COMMIT.
