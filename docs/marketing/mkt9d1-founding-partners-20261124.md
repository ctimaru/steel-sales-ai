## Commercial hypothesis
One commercial program for industrial organizations, distinct from equity fundraising. Italy-first (existing catalog IT scope), EU shown as future interest only until country/category coverage verified. Retain the current exact four keys, names and entitlement meanings: producers (includes Producer Intelligence), distributors, end_users (industrial users), processors (carpentry and processing). Do not bundle unavailable Commercial AI/marketplace features into a priced promise.
Indicative existing monthly IT reference (not approved): 1/2/3/4 modules EUR 39/66/94/119 ex VAT per organization. Proposed founding monthly 29/49/69/89 ex VAT (rounded ~25–27% discount). Twelve months from first real activation, not from interest date; proposed monthly cancellation, no lifetime discount, no stacked annual 15% discount. At renewal, regular then-current price, with notice and applicable contract terms. No price reservation, guarantee, billing, discount entitlement or contract from an expression of interest. Terms, VAT, renewal/cancellation and coverage to be validated before sale. Do not expose reference prices as a previously charged price or claim savings as historical fact.
## Cohort
Start with target 20 pilot organizations, then target 100 activated Founding Partners subject to capacity. No fake countdown, scarcity, remaining slots, customer counters or guaranteed access dates. Initial acquisition through founder-led outreach, existing professional network, LinkedIn, school SEO and company discovery. No unsolicited blast to crawled addresses. Public tools stay accessible; any registration gating uses existing legal/pilot controls, no new bypass.
Illustrative mix: 40% one module, 30% two, 20% three, 10% full. 20 activated paying companies = 8*29 + 6*49 + 4*69 + 2*89 = EUR 980 monthly run rate; 100 = EUR 4,900 MRR / EUR 58,800 annualized run rate (not collected cash, forecast, or equity). Track actual qualified interest, verified contact, accepted pilot, activated org, first payment, 60-day retention and churn separately. Seed remains separate.
